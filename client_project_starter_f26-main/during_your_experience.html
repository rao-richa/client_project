During your Experience

Navigating Your Active Project

The middle of an engaged learning project is where real growth—and real challenges—often occur. Requirements shift, team dynamics evolve, sponsors face unexpected constraints, and research uncovers surprising results.

By managing scope changes, responding to feedback, and aligning technical decisions with human needs, you build resilience, adaptability, and ethical decision-making skills. Friction is not a barrier; it is a valuable part of applied learning.

Managing and Executing in the Field

Conduct research, track progress, and maintain clear communication with project sponsors.

Focus and Objectives

  • Fieldwork and Research: Use human-centered design methods to observe contexts and identify insights.
  • Sponsor and Client Updates: Maintain transparency through regular progress reports.
  • Feedback Loops: Gather, evaluate, and apply constructive feedback throughout the project.
